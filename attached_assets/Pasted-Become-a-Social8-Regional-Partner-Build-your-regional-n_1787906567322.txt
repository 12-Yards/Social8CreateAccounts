What is a Social8 Regional Partner?

A Regional Partner is responsible for growing the Social8 network within an agreed territory.

You identify organisations that could benefit from their own community platform, introduce them to Social8 and help them get started.

Social8 provides the technology, infrastructure and ongoing platform support.

You focus on relationships and growth.

You could work with:
⚽ Sports clubs & fan communities
❤️ Charities & fundraising organisations
🏢 Businesses & professional networks
🎓 Education & student communities
🎟️ Events & entertainment
👥 Membership organisations
🌱 Environmental & local community groups
🎯 Hobby & interest groups
A different kind of partnership
You're not just earning commission on a sale.

Traditional sales roles often mean finding a customer, making a sale and moving on.

With Social8, the opportunity is to build a portfolio of communities.

As the communities within your network generate revenue, you can receive an ongoing share of that revenue.

Build once. Grow continuously.
